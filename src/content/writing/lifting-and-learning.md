I started lifting to get stronger. I kept lifting because it turned out to be the clearest model of learning I have.

## Small loads, every week

Nobody benches 100 kg on day one. You add a little weight, week after week, and the body adapts to just enough stress. Learning works the same way: the useful zone is slightly past what feels comfortable, not far past it.

## Rest is part of the program

Muscle grows during recovery, not during the set. The same is true for memory: the consolidation happens while you sleep. Skipping rest doesn't make you faster, it makes you injured, or in the case of learning, confused.

> The work happens in the gym. The growth happens after.

## Track it or it didn't happen

A training log makes progress visible when the mirror doesn't. Writing these notes is the same idea, applied to thinking.
